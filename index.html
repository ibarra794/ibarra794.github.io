About

I look for the story under the spreadsheet.

I'm trained equally in surveys, statistics, and the slower craft of sitting with people long enough to hear what they actually mean.

My work lives at the intersection of community building, complex problem solving, and data storytelling. I'm most useful when a team has more questions than answers and needs someone to organize the fog.
